MEET THE PEOPLE BEHIND VERIDIAN

Built on Hard Work. Rooted in Our Community

My name is Chris, and I started Veridian Lawn Care with my fiancée because we wanted to build something of our own and provide a service our community could count on. Working in construction has taught me the importance of showing up, paying attention to the details, and taking pride in the work you do. Those are the same principles I bring to every lawn we maintain. Veridian is a small, owner-operated business. I handle the lawn care, while my fiancée helps manage the administrative side and keep things organized behind the scenes. It's just the two of us building this company together, one customer and one property at a time. We're proud to serve homeowners in Jacksonville and the Kernan area. When you choose Veridian, you're supporting a local business built on hard work, personal accountability, and the belief that good service still matters.
